What to look for

Compare small irregular patterns, texture and building edges. Higher smoothing weight penalises differences between neighbouring pixels more strongly. This can reduce noise, but may also soften useful artistic detail.

What to look for

Compare changes in texture, colour and recognisable structure. Look closely at building edges and fine details.

Stronger style influence can produce a more pronounced transformation, but does not automatically make the result more visually appealing.

These examples use one content and style pair. The effects can differ with other images.
